Research & Academic Work

Research Activities & Case Studies

My research interests include sustainable supply chain design, logistics optimization, operational resilience, and mathematical modeling of industrial and urban systems.

FEATURED CASE STUDY

A Two-Stage Clustered MILP Framework for Efficient and Resilient Waste Collection Routing

Developed a two-stage framework combining K-Means clustering and Mixed-Integer Linear Programming to improve municipal solid waste collection routing in Rajshahi City Corporation. The study considers 20 Secondary Transfer Stations and evaluates optimized collection routes, fleet utilization, and operational costs. The proposed model indicates potential operational cost savings of 10.76%–17.52% compared with the evaluated baseline scenarios.
