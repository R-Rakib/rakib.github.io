About the Achievement

Our product design project, the "AI-Based PPE Detection System," competed among innovative submissions at the Orbeetal Idea Contest 2026 during the 11th RCF Career Fair. Organized by the RUET Career Forum (RCF), the competition highlights practical engineering applications, product design, and automated safety solutions.

Securing 4th place reflects the hard work of our university project team in developing a robust computer vision system tailored for industrial safety compliance.
