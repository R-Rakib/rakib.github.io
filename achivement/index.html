PROJECT RECOGNITION

AI-Based PPE Compliance Detection System

Secured 4th place among competitive institutional entries at the Orbeetal Idea Contest during the 11th RCF Career Fair at RUET.

The award-winning project is an edge-AI system developed for industrial safety monitoring. It leverages custom-trained YOLO object detection models deployed on a Raspberry Pi 5 to monitor real-time worker compliance with Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) standards—such as hard hats, safety vests, and eye protection—triggering hardware indicators and visual alerts instantly.
