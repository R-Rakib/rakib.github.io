AI Dury — Image-to-Football Commentary System

Transforming match snapshots into contextual, expressive football commentary using computer vision and Gemini.

Project Demonstration

Project Overview

Football is not just about identifying players or tracking the ball; commentary adds emotion and storytelling. AI Dury is an experimental system combining YOLOv8n object detection with Google Gemini 2.0 Flash to synthesize dramatic match commentary from visual snapshots.

Technologies & Tools:

  • Python, OpenCV & YOLOv8n
  • Google Gemini 2.0 Flash
  • Generative AI & Computer Vision
