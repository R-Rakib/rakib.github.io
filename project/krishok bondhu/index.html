Krishok Bondhu — AI-Powered Rice Disease Assistant

Bilingual AI agricultural assistant pairing a fine-tuned ResNet50 classifier with a Gemini LLM workflow.

Project Demonstration

Project Overview

Krishok Bondhu is a bilingual AI agricultural assistance application designed to identify rice leaf conditions from images using TensorFlow, Keras, ResNet50, and LangGraph workflows connected to Google Gemini.

Technologies & Tools:

  • Python, TensorFlow, Keras & ResNet50
  • Google Gemini & LangGraph
  • Streamlit & SQLite
