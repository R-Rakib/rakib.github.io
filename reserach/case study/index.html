Operational Context & Problem Statement

My case studies explore real-world industrial and operational challenges through analytical thinking, mathematical modelling, and engineering-based problem-solving. My work focuses on understanding complex systems, identifying operational inefficiencies, and developing structured solutions using Operations Research, optimization techniques, and data-driven analysis.

This study investigates the municipal solid waste collection and transportation system of Rajshahi City Corporation (RCC), where inefficient routing and fleet utilization can increase operational costs.

Rather than focusing only on theoretical concepts, I aim to connect engineering principles with practical applications in logistics, supply chain management, resource allocation, and sustainable operations.

The Optimization Framework

We developed a two-stage optimization framework combining K-Means clustering and Mixed Integer Linear Programming (MILP).

The approach partitions 20 Secondary Transfer Stations into four geographical clusters before optimizing waste collection routes and heterogeneous vehicle deployment. The mathematical model accounts for fuel expenditure, driver wages, carbon emission penalties, vehicle capacities, routing feasibility, and an eight-hour working shift.

Key Results & Impact

Across eleven scenarios, the optimized framework achieved estimated daily operational cost reductions of 10.76% – 17.52%.

This demonstrates the significant potential of integrating spatial clustering and mathematical programming for more efficient, sustainable urban waste logistics.
