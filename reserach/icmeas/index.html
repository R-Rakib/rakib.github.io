RESEARCH PAPER

Integrating AHP and Bayesian BWM: A Dual-Phase MCDM Framework

I am honored to have presented our research at ICMEAS 2025, organized by Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST), Dhaka.

Our paper, “Integrating AHP and Bayesian BWM: A Dual-Phase MCDM Framework for Addressing Critical Challenges in Lean Manufacturing Systems”, introduces a dual-phase approach that combines analytical and probabilistic decision-making tools to address key challenges in lean manufacturing.

This milestone marks the beginning of my research journey. It has been an inspiring experience that motivates me to continue exploring advanced methodologies and contribute meaningfully to both academic research and industrial practices.

Conference Highlights

  • Event: ICMEAS 2025
  • Organizer: MIST, Dhaka, Bangladesh
  • Focus: Lean Manufacturing & Multi-Criteria Decision Making (MCDM)
  • Methodology: AHP & Bayesian Best-Worst Method (BWM)
